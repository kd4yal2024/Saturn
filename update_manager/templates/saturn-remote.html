Operator Notes

            
Why this layout

          

          

            

              

                
Persistent Frequency Deck

                
The primary VFO stays dominant even while meters and controls move around it. That keeps the page closer to a real operator console than a generic web dashboard.

              

              

                
Center-First Display

                
The panadapter and waterfall own the middle of the page. Controls are rails around the signal view, not competing hero cards above it.

              

              

                
Browser Advantage

                
Thetis-style density is useful, but this page should be cleaner: faster scan paths, less chrome, and clearer separation between routing, tuning, DSP, and live telemetry.
